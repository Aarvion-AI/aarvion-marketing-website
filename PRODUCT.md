## Platform

web

## Users

Aarvion serves enterprise teams moving AI-agent workflows from pilots into production. The homepage must work for technology and AI leaders, engineering teams, risk and security owners, and operators or auditors who need to understand how an agent reached a consequential action.

## Product Purpose

Aarvion gives enterprises two ways to put agents to work: connect agents they already use, or start with a deployable Cadre agent pack. Both paths run through Aarvion Runtime, which carries routing, policy, authority, approvals, and a signed record across the workflow.

Success means a buyer can understand the relationship between Aarvion Runtime and Cadre within the first viewport, see the product making a consequential decision, and book a demo without decoding a portfolio of separate governance products.

## Positioning

Aarvion is the enterprise agent runtime. It routes work to an approved model or agent, gates consequential actions before they reach enterprise systems, and records the authority chain across handoffs. Cadre supplies deployable agents and blueprints for teams that do not want to begin with a blank agent stack.

## Operating Context

The product sits in the action path between agents and enterprise systems. It supports existing agents, MCP servers, OpenClaw installations, policies, routing rules, approval queues, activity, and provenance. Cadre gives buyers a second on-ramp through deployable agent packs and blueprints; `cadreagents.com` owns the detailed catalog and workflow inventory.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Cadre agent packs are deployable.
- Buyers can also start from a blueprint and deploy it with Aarvion's help.
- The homepage should introduce Cadre as a strategic on-ramp without spending substantial space on individual packs or financial-services workflow examples.
- Customers can connect agents they already run.
- Aarvion Runtime routes steps, evaluates policy and authority, returns PASS, PARK, or BLOCK, supports human approval, and preserves a signed record.
- The homepage's primary conversion is **Book a demo**.
- MCP and OpenClaw are connection modes and integrations, not separate top-level product stories.
- Customer names, benchmarks, certifications, and production results remain unconfirmed for public use and must not be fabricated.

## Brand Commitments

- Aarvion is the master brand. Cadre is an Aarvion product and agent-pack line, not a separate company.
- Use direct, natural language. Avoid generic AI copy, inflated claims, stacked slogans, and deck-like chapter narration.
- The craft bar is the refinement and product theatre of Ramp and Palantir, without copying either company's visual identity or language.
- The experience must feel like a product website: demonstrate the system, vary the pace, and make the route to purchase obvious.

## Product Principles

1. Present two on-ramps and one runtime.
2. Sell the larger move from agent pilots to enterprise production; do not reduce the story to a handful of financial-services workflows.
3. Demonstrate the runtime across agents, models, handoffs, authority, and systems before explaining its parts.
4. Treat governance as part of the runtime, not the company's entire category.
5. Let each section add a new kind of evidence instead of repeating the same argument.
6. Make booking a demo the clearest next step.
